## 2. Business summary

Root Of Lyfe LLC is a business funding-resource, referral, and digital-systems operation. It helps business owners and real-estate investors research financing options, organize information, and connect with third-party funding providers and professional resources.

The company is not a direct lender and does not make underwriting or approval decisions.
