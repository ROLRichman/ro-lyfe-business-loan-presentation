## Questions to ask every lender

1. Is Root Of Lyfe LLC eligible based on its actual business activities and revenue model?
2. Is the financing for business operations, equipment, working capital, or real-estate investment?
3. Are referral or affiliate-based revenues acceptable, and how must they be documented?
4. What are the full costs, including fees and third-party charges?
5. Is a personal guarantee or collateral required?
6. What financial records and tax returns are required?
7. What are the repayment terms and prepayment conditions?
8. Are there restrictions on use of proceeds?
9. Is the provider a direct lender, broker, marketplace, or referral network?
10. Is any compensation paid to a broker or referral source, and how is it disclosed?

## Comparison principles

- Compare APR and total repayment cost, not only the advertised rate.
- Confirm the exact borrower and guarantor.
- Match the financing product to the intended use.
- Avoid stacking financing without understanding the total obligations.
- Do not pay upfront fees without independently verifying the provider and the service.
- Do not rely on verbal promises; obtain material terms in writing.
