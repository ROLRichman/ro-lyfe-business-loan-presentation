Company overview

Root Of Lyfe LLC helps business owners and real-estate investors research financing pathways, organize funding needs, and connect with appropriate third-party funding providers and professional resources. The company also develops digital resources and business workflows.

Role and disclosure: Root Of Lyfe LLC is a funding/referral and business-resource operation. It is not a direct lender, bank, realtor, or grant-making agency. Third-party providers independently determine eligibility, underwriting, terms, and approvals. No funding outcome is guaranteed.

Business development direction

  • Maintain a curated business funding and grant resource directory.
  • Develop transparent third-party referral and resource-navigation workflows.
  • Improve digital systems, business operations, and customer readiness.
  • Evaluate tax-deed real-estate opportunities only when suitable capital, due diligence, and lender approval support the transaction.

Important information for lenders

This site is a business presentation and preparation resource, not a completed loan application or financial statement. Financial figures, requested amount, use of proceeds, and repayment capacity must be completed from verified records before submission.
