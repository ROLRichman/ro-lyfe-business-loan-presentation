## Tax-deed acquisition strategy

If suitable funding becomes available, the user intends to pursue tax-deed properties. The user reports having contractors, realtors, lenders, underwriters, a loan officer, and a coach in their professional network.

This is a planned strategy, not proof of committed capital, a property purchase, or guaranteed financing. The workflow is: identify properties; review auction rules; research parcel, title, liens, taxes, and legal risks; inspect condition; obtain contractor estimates; evaluate comparable sales and value; calculate maximum bid and total costs; obtain appropriate lender review; bid only when due diligence and capital support it.

Do not assume a business grant or general-purpose operating loan can fund tax-deed acquisition. Confirm permitted use with the lender.
