## 1. Executive overview

Root Of Lyfe LLC is a Pennsylvania limited liability company focused on business funding resources, financing referrals, real-estate funding navigation, and digital business systems. The company helps business owners and real-estate investors research financing pathways, organize funding needs, and connect with appropriate third-party lenders and service providers.

Root Of Lyfe LLC operates as a broker/referral and business-resource operation. **It is not represented as a bank, direct lender, mortgage lender, realtor, or grant-making agency.** Financing decisions, underwriting, terms, approvals, and disbursements are made by the applicable third-party provider.

The company is developing organized digital resources and workflows to support its business operations, partner relationships, and customer education.
