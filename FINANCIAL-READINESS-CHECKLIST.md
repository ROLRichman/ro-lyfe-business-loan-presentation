# Root Of Lyfe LLC — Financial Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist to prepare for a business financing discussion. A checked item means the document has been located and reviewed; it does not mean the lender has accepted it.

## 1. Entity and ownership

- [ ] Pennsylvania formation document.
- [ ] Current Pennsylvania entity status / subsistence record.
- [ ] Operating agreement.
- [ ] Ownership and beneficial-owner information.
- [ ] EIN confirmation letter (secure delivery only).
- [ ] Business licenses, registrations, and tax clearances as applicable.
- [ ] Verified business and mailing addresses.
- [ ] Business contact details.

## 2. Financial records

- [ ] Last 2–3 years of business tax returns, if available and requested.
- [ ] Current year-to-date profit-and-loss statement.
- [ ] Current balance sheet.
- [ ] Business bank statements for the lender's requested period.
- [ ] Accounts receivable and payable summary, if applicable.
- [ ] Debt schedule with balances, payments, and maturity dates.
- [ ] Revenue detail by source.
- [ ] Owner compensation / distributions, if applicable.
- [ ] Explanation of unusual deposits or expenses.
- [ ] Bookkeeping reconciled to bank statements.

## 3. Revenue substantiation

For referral and affiliate income, preserve:
- Executed partner or affiliate terms.
- Referral records and status.
- Commission statements.
- Payment confirmations and bank deposits.
- Invoices and service agreements, where applicable.
- Refunds, reversals, chargebacks, and unpaid amounts.

Do not count pending leads, possible deals, or advertised partner funding limits as revenue.

## 4. Loan request

- [ ] Specific financing amount.
- [ ] Written project purpose.
- [ ] Itemized use-of-funds budget.
- [ ] Vendor estimates / quotes.
- [ ] Project timeline.
- [ ] Owner contribution evidence, if applicable.
- [ ] Confirmation that the lender permits the intended use.
- [ ] Repayment source and cash-flow analysis.

## 5. Credit, guarantees, and collateral

Complete only as requested by the lender:
- [ ] Business credit information.
- [ ] Personal credit authorization, if required.
- [ ] Personal financial statement, if required.
- [ ] Guarantees and co-borrower details, if applicable.
- [ ] Collateral schedule and ownership evidence, if applicable.
- [ ] Existing liens and obligations.

Do not place Social Security numbers, full account numbers, identity documents, or credit reports in this repository.

## 6. Business plan and operations

- [ ] Final company profile.
- [ ] Service descriptions and customer workflow.
- [ ] Pricing and revenue model.
- [ ] Partner / referral disclosures.
- [ ] Marketing and growth plan.
- [ ] Key risks and mitigation plan.
- [ ] Staffing and contractor plan, if applicable.
- [ ] Digital asset and technology plan.

## 7. Secure document handling

This repository is public. Store only checklists, blank templates, and non-sensitive narrative here.

Send actual financial records only through a verified lender portal or approved secure channel. Never commit EIN letters, tax returns, bank statements, IDs, Social Security numbers, passwords, customer records, or private partner data.
